Tatra Labs

I build AI systems that combine language models with explicit inference and search — and I measure whether the combination actually earns its keep.

Most of my work sits where a model's output meets a constraint it has to satisfy: a crossword grid that must agree with itself, a labour-market chart that must not claim a difference its own margins of error cannot support. I care about calibration, honest evaluation, and saying plainly what a result does not show. Everything here is source-available and reproducible.
