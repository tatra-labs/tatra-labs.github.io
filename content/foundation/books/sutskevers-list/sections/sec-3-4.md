**Fisher Yu, Vladlen Koltun · 2016 · [arXiv:1511.07122](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.07122)**

Segmentation networks in 2015 were classification networks with the head cut off, and a classification stack is built to destroy resolution — the right trade when the output is one label, the wrong one when it is a label per pixel. VGG-16 pools five times, so a `224x224` input leaves a `7x7` map. The fixes were retroactive: FCN upsampled the coarse map and added skip connections from earlier, finer layers; others ran the image at several scales and fused. Both recovered detail after it had been thrown away, and both bought context with resolution — the only way to widen a unit's view was to shrink the map underneath it.

Yu and Koltun, at ICLR 2016, separated the two. The `l`-dilated convolution is `(F *_l k)(p) = Σ_{s + l·t = p} F(s)·k(t)`: the kernel samples the input at stride `l` between taps instead of at adjacent positions. A `3x3` kernel with `l=4` covers a `9x9` footprint using the same nine weights and returns a map the size of its input. Stacked with doubling dilation the footprints compose — after layers of dilation `1, 2, 4, ..., 2^i`, each unit sees `(2^(i+2) - 1) x (2^(i+2) - 1)`. Receptive field grows exponentially in depth, parameters linearly, resolution not at all.

They shipped it as two parts. A front end: delete the last two pooling and striding layers from VGG-16, dilate the convolutions that followed them by 2 and 4 so their footprints are unchanged, and drop the padding on intermediate maps. Then a bolt-on context module — eight layers, seven `3x3` with dilations `1, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 1` and a closing `1x1` — carrying the receptive field to `67x67` without touching resolution. The module needed identity initialization to train: centre tap 1 on the matching channel, everything else 0, so it begins as a pass-through and learns only a departure from it. The front end alone reached 67.6 mean IoU on VOC-2012 test against 62.2 for FCN-8s and 62.1 for DeepLab, most of that from deletion rather than addition. On validation the context module moved 69.8 to 71.3 (basic) and 72.1 (large).

Dilation is now the standard way to widen context in a dense-prediction network. It went into DeepLab's atrous spatial pyramid pooling, into dilated residual networks, and out of vision entirely — WaveNet's stack of causal dilated convolutions over raw audio is the same operator on a 1-D signal. The operator itself was older, borrowed from the à trous algorithm in wavelet decomposition. What this paper contributed was the exponential stack and the demonstration that the classification scaffolding could simply be removed.

> Receptive field and resolution are independent knobs, and only pooling ever tied them together.

**The caveat:** gridding. A unit computed with dilation `l` reads only inputs whose coordinates match its own modulo `l`, so a stack of layers whose dilations share a common factor splits into `l²` populations that never mix, and thin structures come out checkered. The paper did not notice. Yu's own follow-up, Dilated Residual Networks, added degridding layers to undo it; later work staggered dilations to be coprime. The serial context module did not last either — parallel dilated branches and encoder-decoder skips replaced it.
